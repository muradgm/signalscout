# SignalScout Website Inspiration Research

## Objective

Reset the landing page toward a more meaningful, less AI-coded direction.

The target feeling is:

- calm
- premium
- architectural
- editorial
- sharply product-led
- not generic AI SaaS

This research is meant to guide accurate decisions on:

- section sizing
- padding rhythm
- text hierarchy
- color restraint
- motion style
- hero composition

## Core References

### 1. Mercury

Source: [Mercury homepage](https://mercury.com/)

What stands out:

- very open hero spacing
- large simple headline with a short direct subhead
- one primary product image moment, not a pile of cards
- polished, premium trust posture without theatrical motion
- section rhythm feels expensive because there is room around each idea

What to borrow:

- large whitespace between page movements
- fewer competing surfaces
- clearer hero-to-proof-to-product progression
- more confidence in simple composition

What not to copy literally:

- fintech-specific framing
- startup-banking visuals

### 2. Stripe

Source: [Stripe homepage](https://stripe.com/)

What stands out:

- commanding headline scale
- very clear value statement
- strong contrast and disciplined color use
- crisp statistical proof moments
- page feels highly designed without needing lots of decorative components

What to borrow:

- stronger headline authority
- clearer proof architecture
- sharper section framing
- tighter CTA confidence

What not to copy literally:

- infrastructure-scale language
- heavy enterprise density

### 3. Resend

Source: [Resend homepage](https://www.resend.com/)

What stands out:

- clarity over spectacle
- quick product understanding
- soft, tasteful visual treatment
- clean surfaces with minimal clutter
- homepage explains one thing well before expanding

What to borrow:

- calm product-first storytelling
- lighter chrome
- less decorative UI density
- a cleaner relationship between copy and product visual

What not to copy literally:

- developer-product coding motifs
- too much "tool demo" energy

### 4. Raycast

Source: [Raycast homepage](https://www.raycast.com/)

What stands out:

- motion and interaction feel intentional
- strong brand confidence without over-decoration
- product feels fast because composition and pacing are crisp
- copy is concise and emotional at the same time

What to borrow:

- controlled visible motion
- better emotional sharpness in the hero
- more conviction in the statement layer

What not to copy literally:

- keyboard-product motifs
- OS utility framing

### 5. Framer

Source: [Framer homepage](https://www.framer.com/)

What stands out:

- motion is visible quickly
- typography leads the page
- sections feel designed as moments, not stacked features

What to borrow:

- stronger motion visibility
- section-as-scene mindset
- better pacing between headline, product visual, and proof

What not to copy literally:

- design-tool aesthetic excess
- generic "creative builder" gradients

## Direction Decision

Recommended blend:

- Mercury for page rhythm and elegance
- Stripe for hierarchy and confidence
- Resend for cleanliness
- Raycast for restrained visible motion

SignalScout should not feel like:

- AI automation theater
- futuristic glowing product marketing
- dashboard cards pasted into a hero
- a tool catalog

SignalScout should feel like:

- a well-composed decision system
- product authority without noise
- selective, careful, intelligent review

## Page-Level Corrections

### 1. Section Spacing

The page needs bigger vertical rhythm.

Recommended desktop spacing:

- between major sections: `160px`
- hero top padding below nav: `88px` to `112px`
- section heading to section body: `40px` to `56px`
- internal content gaps in complex sections: `24px` to `32px`

Recommended mobile spacing:

- between major sections: `96px` to `112px`
- heading to body: `24px` to `32px`

### 2. Content Width

Recommended structure:

- outer shell max width: `1320px`
- reading width for long copy: `560px` to `620px`
- hero copy width: around `540px`
- product-stage content width should not feel edge-to-edge

### 3. Hero Composition

The hero should read in this order:

1. statement
2. supporting explanation
3. CTA
4. one dominant product-art moment

Rules:

- no overlap that blocks the headline
- no floating-panel pile
- one main visual system with one or two secondary accents only
- motion should be visible within the first 2 seconds

## Typography Guidance

### What the type should feel like

- more authoritative than startup-friendly
- more editorial than software-default
- clean, modern, and quiet

### Recommended hierarchy shape

- eyebrow: slightly larger, bold, tracking-led
- hero headline: large, crisp, serious
- body text: low-friction, highly readable, not too geometric
- proof labels: compact and sharp

### Practical guidance

- keep the serif display for major headlines if it still feels refined
- use the sans body as the stabilizer, not the personality overload
- limit long paragraphs to roughly `12-16` words per line on desktop where possible

## Color Guidance

The current direction should become more controlled, not more colorful.

Recommended palette behavior:

- base background: cool porcelain / pale mist
- text: deep graphite, not pure black
- accent: one restrained cobalt or electric blue
- secondary accent: very quiet mineral green only if needed

Rules:

- avoid obvious AI blues and neon glows
- avoid too many gradient moments
- use contrast to guide trust, not decoration

## Motion Guidance

Motion should feel:

- deliberate
- architectural
- slow enough to feel premium
- visible enough to actually register

Recommended motion system:

- one major hero motion
- one subtle secondary motion layer
- one reveal rhythm for sections

Avoid:

- too many independently floating cards
- decorative drifting everywhere
- motion that feels like screensaver behavior

## Implications For The Live Landing Page

### Keep

- full-width nav
- calmer overall direction
- tighter brand hierarchy
- reduced shadow treatment

### Change next

- simplify the hero into one stronger visual statement
- reduce the number of small hero fragments
- make motion more structural, not ornamental
- give each section a more distinct scene-like identity
- tighten text so each section says one thing with conviction

### Best next design pass

1. Hero restructuring
2. Section rhythm and spacing pass
3. Copy tightening pass
4. Color restraint pass
5. Motion refinement after structure is right

## PM Recommendation

The homepage should move toward:

`Mercury rhythm + Stripe hierarchy + Resend clarity + Raycast motion restraint`

That is the strongest path to something that feels more meaningful, more original, and less like an AI startup template.
