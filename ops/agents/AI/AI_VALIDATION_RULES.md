# AI VALIDATION RULES

An output fails validation if any of the following is true:
- Output contains claims unsupported by evidence.
- Audit issues are generic enough to fit almost any business.
- Recommendations do not map to identified issues.
- Outreach uses manipulative, spammy, or inflated language.
- Confidence is presented as high despite weak evidence.
- Outreach claims are not bounded by accepted audit context.
- Outreach includes fabricated specifics or unsupported certainty.
- Weak-contact or incomplete-evidence cases are written as high-confidence outreach.
- Output implies broader send/reply maturity than current truth supports.

## Minimum Acceptance Standard
- Output matches the assignment packet.
- Output matches the requested contract or review format.
- Risks and uncertainty are stated honestly.
- Claims are traceable to truth files, evidence, or verified execution results.
- Outreach claim -> evidence -> CTA mapping is explicit and checkable for outreach-lane tasks.

## Preferred Quality
- concise
- grounded
- operator-useful
- easy for PM and QA to compare against the packet
