# SignalScout Logo Review Scorecard

## Purpose

Use this scorecard to compare first-round logo concepts side by side.

This should help us make decisions based on:

- trust
- clarity
- fit
- scalability
- brand quality

Not just on which concept feels the most novel in isolation.

## Review Rule

Score each concept from `1` to `5` in every category.

- `1` = weak
- `2` = below bar
- `3` = workable but not convincing
- `4` = strong
- `5` = excellent

Any concept that scores below `4` on:

- trust
- small-size clarity
- navbar fit

should not advance.

## Concept Info

For each concept, record:

- concept name
- direction family
- version
- source prompt/spec
- reviewer notes

## Score Categories

### 1. Trust

Question:

Does this feel credible enough for a product making evidence-led recommendations?

Look for:

- seriousness
- calm authority
- no gimmick energy

### 2. Originality

Question:

Does this feel ownable, or does it look like a generic SaaS or analytics mark?

Look for:

- distinct silhouette
- non-generic construction
- memorability without weirdness

### 3. Precision

Question:

Does the mark feel controlled, deliberate, and structurally clean?

Look for:

- clean geometry
- balanced spacing
- no awkward stroke logic

### 4. Premium Feel

Question:

Does this feel refined enough for a polished modern software brand?

Look for:

- restraint
- elegance
- confidence

### 5. Navbar Fit

Question:

Does this improve the website navbar immediately?

Look for:

- strong left-lockup presence
- good balance with navigation links
- clean brand hierarchy

### 6. Wordmark Balance

Question:

Does the icon support the wordmark instead of overpowering it?

Look for:

- proportional lockup
- readable brand name
- no icon dominance

### 7. Small-Size Clarity

Question:

Does the icon still hold up at favicon or app-icon scale?

Look for:

- readable silhouette
- no fragile detail
- recognizability in reduction

### 8. Homepage Fit

Question:

Does this match the current website direction: Stripe-sharp, Mercury-elegant, Resend-clean?

Look for:

- visual tone alignment
- no mismatch with page typography and layout

### 9. Motion Potential

Question:

Could this support future subtle hero or logo motion?

Look for:

- clean reveal path
- scan/sweep potential
- no need for flashy animation

## Red-Flag Checks

Mark any concept that triggers one of these:

- looks like a chart icon
- looks like a magnifying glass
- looks like a security/cyber mark
- looks too much like a stock logo
- becomes weak in black and white
- only works because of styling

If any red flag is strong, the concept should be dropped even with decent scores.

## Shortlist Rule

Advance only concepts that:

- average at least `4.0`
- score `4` or better on trust
- score `4` or better on navbar fit
- score `4` or better on small-size clarity
- trigger no major red flags

## PM Decision Rule

When two concepts are close:

- choose the one with stronger trust
- then choose the one with better navbar fit
- then choose the one with better small-size clarity

Do not choose the more clever concept over the more durable concept.
