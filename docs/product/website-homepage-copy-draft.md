## Copy Principles

All homepage copy should:

- stay inside the single-operator wedge
- emphasize review, evidence, and judgment
- treat sending as real but secondary
- keep replies secondary
- avoid generic AI-sales language
- sound clean, specific, and composed

Do not use copy that implies:

- autonomous outbound
- end-to-end reply operations
- full platform breadth
- broad team workflow
- universal fit across all local businesses
