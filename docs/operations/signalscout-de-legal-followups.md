# SignalScout.de Legal Follow-Ups

Status: optional / draft later

This note captures the additional legal documents that may be needed if the public site scope expands beyond a simple informational site.

## Draft only if these features exist

### Cookie / Consent notice

Needed if the site uses:
- analytics
- marketing pixels
- embedded maps
- video embeds
- chat widgets
- any non-essential cookies or similar technologies

### Newsletter / marketing consent text

Needed if the site collects email addresses for newsletters or marketing updates.

### AGB / service terms and Widerrufsbelehrung

Needed if the site sells services or subscriptions to consumers online.

### Processor / compliance records

Usually internal rather than public-facing, but needed if the site uses processors or external infrastructure in scope.

## Current assumption

If signalscout.de remains a simple informational site with only contact and technically necessary functionality, no additional public legal pages are required beyond:
- Impressum
- Datenschutzerklärung
- optional Nutzungsbedingungen

## Reminder

Revisit this note before adding analytics, marketing tools, consumer checkout, or any other non-essential tracking features.
